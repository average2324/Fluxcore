### Description

```
FluxCore is a reflex arcade game about piloting a ship through a collapsing ring of hazards. Read the gap, commit to the turn, and survive one more rotation.

A CAMPAIGN, NOT AN ENDLESS LOOP
100 hand-tuned levels grouped into 10 themed zones, each with its own palette, hazard set and escalation curve:
Dock Departure, Asteroid Belt, Lagrange Drift, Deep Orbit, Fracture Zone, Command Burn, War Horizon, Bio Defense, Nova Ambush and Kitchen Rush.
Every level has a designed pattern rhythm rather than pure randomness, and a level-select map lets you replay any stage you have reached.

SHIPS TO EARN
Collect coins by clearing levels and unlock 11 ships, from the starting Specter-7 to Orion Prime. Each hull has its own silhouette and collision profile, so the ship you fly changes how tight a gap you can thread.

TOOLS FOR HARD RUNS
Stock consumable shields to absorb one hit, or trigger time-slow to stretch a moment you would otherwise lose. Both are earned in-game.

DIFFICULTY THAT FITS YOU
Relaxed, Standard and Expert modes adjust both hazard speed and readability, so the game can be a calm skill trainer or a punishing test.

BUILT FOR PICK-UP-AND-PLAY
One-thumb controls. Instant restarts. A guided tutorial for the opening levels. Full English and Turkish localisation. Everything runs offline, with no account and no sign-up, and your progress is stored on your own device.

FLUXCORE PREMIUM
The iOS version contains no advertising of any kind. A single, optional, non-consumable App Store purchase unlocks FluxCore Premium with unlimited lives. It is a one-time unlock, not a subscription, and the full campaign is playable without it.
```

## Dikkat

- **Ekran görüntüleri.** Türkçe eklersen o dil için de ekran görüntüsü istenebilir. Aynı
  görselleri kullanabilirsin ama Türkçe arayüzle çekersen çok daha iyi olur — oyun zaten
  tam Türkçe. Ayarlardan dili Türkçe yapıp yeniden çek.
- **iPad görüntüleri şart.** İnceleme **iPad Air 11" (M3)** üzerinde yapılıyor. iPad
  ekran görüntülerin gerçek oyundan ve düzgün olsun.
- **Bölge adlarını çevirme.** "Dock Departure" gibi adlar oyunun içinde İngilizce
  görünüyor, açıklamada da öyle kalsın ki tutarlı olsun.
- **Copyright alanı** `2026 Lumina Digitale` olarak duruyor. Geliştirici hesabı adıyla
  uyumlu olduğundan emin ol.
- Değişiklikten sonra **Save**'e bas. Sürüm reddedilmiş durumda olduğu için metadata
  düzenlemesi serbest ve yeniden inceleme başlatmaz.
