FluxCore is an original single-player reflex arcade game developed by us. It is not based
on an app template, a purchased project, or a reskin.

Asset ownership in this build:
- All music and sound effects are original work, synthesised in-house for this app.
- All in-game graphics (ships, HUD icons, effects, backgrounds, level visuals) are
  generated procedurally at runtime by our own rendering code. No sprite sheets, icon
  packs, stock art or emoji artwork are bundled.
- The only third-party asset in the app is the Noto Sans typeface (SIL Open Font License),
  used for multilingual text rendering.

Regarding the previous 4.3(a) rejection of build 16: that build bundled two widely
redistributed Creative Commons music tracks and three Twemoji-derived icons. Those files
have been removed in this build and replaced with original in-house audio and procedural
vector icons, so the binary no longer shares file content with any other app.

Development history: this game has been in closed testing on Google Play under our own
developer account since 31 March 2026 (Android package com.orbitflux), with six releases
through 22 July 2026. Play Console records are available on request.

The app has no account system, no user-generated content, and no server backend. All
progress is stored locally on device. The optional in-app purchase (FluxCore Premium,
fluxcore_premium) is a one-time non-consumable unlock, not a subscription. No advertising
SDK is present in the iOS build.
