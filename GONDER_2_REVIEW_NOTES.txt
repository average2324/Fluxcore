FluxCore is an original single-player reflex arcade game developed by us. It is not based
on an app template, a purchased project, an app generator, or a reskin of another app.

WHAT IS IN THE APP

This is a full campaign game, not a single endless loop:
- 100 designed levels, grouped into 10 themed zones (Dock Departure, Asteroid Belt,
  Lagrange Drift, Deep Orbit, Fracture Zone, Command Burn, War Horizon, Bio Defense,
  Nova Ambush, Kitchen Rush). Each zone has its own palette, hazard set and pacing.
- A level-select map for replaying any reached stage.
- 11 unlockable ships bought with an in-game soft currency earned by clearing levels.
  Each ship has a different collision profile that changes gameplay.
- Consumable shields and a time-slow ability, both earned in game.
- Three difficulty modes that adjust hazard speed and readability.
- A guided tutorial, a lives system, and full English and Turkish localisation.

ASSET OWNERSHIP IN THIS BUILD

- All music and sound effects are original work, synthesised in-house for this app.
- All in-game graphics (ships, HUD icons, effects, backgrounds, level visuals) are
  generated procedurally at runtime by our own rendering code. No sprite sheets, icon
  packs, stock art, or emoji artwork are bundled.
- The only third-party asset in the app is the Noto Sans typeface (SIL Open Font License),
  used for multilingual text rendering.

REGARDING THE PREVIOUS 4.3(a) REJECTION OF BUILD 16

Build 16 bundled two widely redistributed Creative Commons music tracks ("Voxel Revolution"
and "Mesmerizing Galaxy" by Kevin MacLeod) and three Twemoji-derived UI icons. Those files
appear unmodified in a large number of unrelated published apps, so our binary shared exact
file content with them. We removed all of these files in this build and replaced them with
original in-house audio and procedural vector icons. The binary no longer shares file
content with any other app.

DEVELOPMENT HISTORY

This game has been in closed testing on Google Play under our own developer account since
31 March 2026 (Android package com.orbitflux), with six releases through 22 July 2026.
Play Console records and screenshots are available on request.

TECHNICAL NOTES FOR REVIEW

- The app has no account system, no sign-up, no user-generated content, and no server
  backend. All progress is stored locally on device.
- No advertising SDK is present in the iOS build.
- The optional in-app purchase (FluxCore Premium, product ID fluxcore_premium) is a
  one-time non-consumable unlock granting unlimited lives. It is not a subscription, and
  the full 100-level campaign is playable without purchasing it.
- Restore Purchases is available in the Premium screen and is user-triggered.
- The app is portrait-only and supports iPhone and iPad.
