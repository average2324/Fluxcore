# Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 30, 2026

FluxCore stores gameplay settings, language choice, best score, and progression on-device.

The Android app currently includes Google Mobile Ads (banner and rewarded ads) and may include Google Play Billing for the optional one-time FluxCore Premium purchase. The iOS app does not include ads in the initial App Store build and may use Apple StoreKit for the optional one-time FluxCore Premium purchase. These services may process technical and transaction-related data as described by their providers, including:
- Device and app information
- Approximate network and request metadata (for example IP-derived region)
- Advertising identifiers and ad interaction events
- Purchase state, transaction tokens, and billing-related verification metadata when premium billing is used

FluxCore does not require account creation and does not collect direct personal profile fields (such as name/email) from players in normal gameplay flow.

Consent handling:
- On supported regions, ad consent is requested through Google User Messaging Platform (UMP).
- If consent is unavailable, ads are not requested.

Third-party notices remain available in `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` and in the in-app legal screens.

If analytics, new crash-reporting services, or additional networked features are added in future releases, this policy must be updated before release and reflected in store disclosures.
