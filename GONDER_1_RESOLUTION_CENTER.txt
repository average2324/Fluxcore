A LIKELY CAUSE, WHICH WE HAVE REMOVED

Rather than only disputing the rejection, we audited our bundle for files that could be
byte-identical to other submitted apps. We found four, and removed all of them in build 17:

1. Two Creative Commons music tracks, "Voxel Revolution" and "Mesmerizing Galaxy" by Kevin
MacLeod. These files are redistributed very widely and appear unmodified in a large number
of published apps. Bundling them made our binary share exact file content with many
unrelated apps, plausibly including apps from the account you matched us against. They are
replaced with original music we synthesised in-house.

2. Three UI icons derived from the open-source Twemoji set. All HUD icons are now drawn at
runtime by our own vector code.

3. A tap indicator adapted from an open-source UI snippet, now drawn procedurally.

4. An unused bundled image.

The only third-party asset left in FluxCore is the Noto Sans typeface (SIL Open Font
License). Every visual element is generated at runtime by our own rendering code, and all
music and sound effects are our own original work. We also renamed internal identifiers
that still carried this project's earlier working title.
